Tick each topic off once you can explain it without notes. The "Must know" facts are the ones questions turn on. Each lesson link goes to the complete, free lesson.

### Domain 1: People

**Weight: 33%.** Leading and engaging people: a shared vision, conflict, team leadership, stakeholder engagement and expectations, knowledge transfer and communication. Official weighting 33%.

📝 Revision notes: [Domain 1: People](notes/domain-1-people.md)

- [ ] **Develop a common vision**
  <br>Enablers: Help ensure a shared vision with key stakeholders; Promote the shared vision; Keep the vision current; Break down situations to identify the root cause of a misunderstanding of the vision.
  - 📖 Lesson: [PMP Develop a Common Vision: Shared Vision, Ownership and Root Cause](https://www.savemycert.com/revision/pmp-project-management-professional/pmp-develop-common-project-vision/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=pmp-study-guide)
  - Must know: The vision is the why; scope is the what. Every trade-off is tested against the intended outcome, on predictive and agile projects alike.
  - Must know: The charter formally records purpose, measurable objectives and success criteria and authorizes the project; a product vision statement, product box and roadmap carry the vision on agile work.
- [ ] **Manage conflicts**
  <br>Enablers: Identify conflict sources; Analyze the context for the conflict; Implement an agreed-on resolution strategy; Communicate conflict management principles with the team and external stakeholders; Establish an environment that fosters adherence to common ground rules; Manage and rectify ground rule violations.
  - 📖 Lesson: [PMP Conflict Management: Techniques, Levels and Ground Rules](https://www.savemycert.com/revision/pmp-project-management-professional/pmp-conflict-management-techniques/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=pmp-study-guide)
  - Must know: Find the cause before choosing a response: personality clashes often hide resource, priority or role conflicts.
  - Must know: Context decides the technique: importance, urgency, relationships, authority and culture.
- [ ] **Lead the project team**
  <br>Enablers: Establish expectations at the team level; Empower the team; Solve problems; Represent the voice of the team; Support the team's varied experiences, skills, and perspectives; Determine an appropriate leadership style; Establish clear roles and responsibilities within the team.
  - 📖 Lesson: [Leading the Project Team for the PMP: Leadership Styles, Empowerment and Team Roles](https://www.savemycert.com/revision/pmp-project-management-professional/pmp-lead-project-team-leadership-styles-empowerment/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=pmp-study-guide)
  - Must know: The team writes its own charter or working agreement; the project manager facilitates it and revisits it with the team whenever members, locations or work change.
  - Must know: When someone misses a team expectation: talk privately to understand the cause, clarify, agree a specific improvement plan, and escalate only if that fails.
- [ ] **Engage stakeholders**
  <br>Enablers: Identify stakeholders; Analyze stakeholders; Analyze and tailor communication to stakeholder needs; Execute the stakeholder engagement plan; Optimize alignment among stakeholder needs, expectations, and project objectives; Build trust and influence stakeholders to accomplish project objectives.
  - 📖 Lesson: [PMP Stakeholder Engagement: Identify, Analyze, Engage and Build Trust](https://www.savemycert.com/revision/pmp-project-management-professional/pmp-stakeholder-engagement-analysis/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=pmp-study-guide)
  - Must know: Stakeholder engagement is a loop: identify, analyze, plan, execute, then reassess whenever something changes.
  - Must know: Identify stakeholders as soon as the charter exists, using many sources (existing stakeholders, lessons learned, experts), and keep identifying them all project long.
- [ ] **Align stakeholder expectations**
  <br>Enablers: Categorize stakeholders; Identify stakeholder expectations; Facilitate discussions to align expectations; Organize and act on mentoring opportunities.
  - 📖 Lesson: [PMP Align Stakeholder Expectations: Categorize, Facilitate and Mentor](https://www.savemycert.com/revision/pmp-project-management-professional/pmp-align-stakeholder-expectations/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=pmp-study-guide)
  - Must know: Power/interest grid: manage closely (high/high), keep satisfied (high power, low interest), keep informed (low power, high interest), monitor (low/low).
  - Must know: Salience model: power, legitimacy and urgency; all three make a definitive stakeholder, legitimacy plus urgency without power make a dependent one.
- [ ] **Manage stakeholder expectations**
  <br>Enablers: Identify internal and external customer expectations; Align and maintain outcomes to internal and external customer expectations; Monitor internal and external customer satisfaction/expectations and respond as needed.
  - 📖 Lesson: [PMP Manage Stakeholder Expectations: Customer Expectations, Alignment and Satisfaction](https://www.savemycert.com/revision/pmp-project-management-professional/pmp-manage-stakeholder-customer-expectations/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=pmp-study-guide)
  - Must know: External customers are outside the organization and receive or use the outcome; internal customers operate, support or work with it inside the organization, and their readiness expectations are part of success.
  - Must know: Gather expectations from the real customers and users (interviews plus observation) before requirements are written, reaching them through any contractually agreed contact.
- [ ] **Help ensure knowledge transfer**
  <br>Enablers: Identify knowledge critical to the project; Gather knowledge; Foster an environment for knowledge transfer.
  - 📖 Lesson: [PMP Knowledge Transfer: Identify, Gather and Share Critical Knowledge](https://www.savemycert.com/revision/pmp-project-management-professional/pmp-knowledge-transfer-project-management/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=pmp-study-guide)
  - Must know: Knowledge transfer has three enablers: identify critical knowledge, gather it, and foster an environment where people share it.
  - Must know: Explicit knowledge goes into repositories and documents; tacit knowledge moves through shadowing, pairing, coaching and joint work, not manuals or courses.
- [ ] **Plan and manage communication**
  <br>Enablers: Define a communication strategy; Promote transparency and collaboration; Establish a feedback loop; Understand reporting requirements; Create reports aligned with sponsors and stakeholder expectations; Support reporting and governance processes.
  - 📖 Lesson: [PMP Communication Management: Plan, Channels, Reports and Governance](https://www.savemycert.com/revision/pmp-project-management-professional/pmp-project-communication-management/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=pmp-study-guide)
  - Must know: Analyze each stakeholder group's information needs first; tools, templates and meetings come after.
  - Must know: Communication channels = n(n − 1) ÷ 2, counting the project manager; for a change in team size, subtract the two totals.

### Domain 2: Process

**Weight: 41%.** Delivering the work: integrated planning and delivery approach, scope, value-based delivery, resources, procurement, finance, quality, schedule, status and closure. Official weighting 41%.

📝 Revision notes: [Domain 2: Process](notes/domain-2-process.md)

- [ ] **Develop an integrated project management plan and plan delivery**
  <br>Enablers: Assess project needs, complexity, and magnitude; Recommend a project management development approach (i.e., predictive, adaptive/agile, or hybrid management); Determine critical information requirements (e.g., sustainability); Recommend a project execution strategy; Create an integrated project management plan; Estimate work effort and resource requirements; Assess consolidated project plans for dependencies, gaps, and continued business value; Maintain the integrated project management plan; Collect and analyze data to make informed project decisions.
  - 📖 Lesson: [PMP Integrated Project Management Plan: Approach, Estimating and Plan Delivery](https://www.savemycert.com/revision/pmp-project-management-professional/pmp-integrated-project-management-plan/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=pmp-study-guide)
  - Must know: Magnitude is size; complexity is uncertainty. Requirements and technical uncertainty, not budget or headcount, drive the delivery approach.
  - Must know: Predictive = fixed requirements, single delivery; iterative = evolving requirements, single delivery; incremental = known, stable-enough requirements, frequent delivery; agile = both evolving and frequent.
- [ ] **Develop and manage project scope**
  <br>Enablers: Define scope; Obtain stakeholder agreement on project scope; Break down scope.
  - 📖 Lesson: [PMP Scope Management: Requirements, Scope Statement, WBS and Backlog](https://www.savemycert.com/revision/pmp-project-management-professional/pmp-develop-manage-project-scope/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=pmp-study-guide)
  - Must know: Pick the elicitation technique by the problem: can't describe the work, observe; can't specify the product, prototype; groups conflict under a deadline, run a facilitated workshop.
  - Must know: The scope statement holds the product scope description, deliverables, acceptance criteria and exclusions; the charter authorizes and the scope management plan describes the method.
- [ ] **Help ensure value-based delivery**
  <br>Enablers: Identify value components with key stakeholders; Prioritize work based on value and stakeholder feedback; Assess opportunities to deliver value incrementally; Examine the business value throughout the project; Verify a measurement system is in place to track benefits; Evaluate delivery options to demonstrate value.
  - 📖 Lesson: [PMP Value-Based Delivery: Prioritization, Incremental Value and Benefits](https://www.savemycert.com/revision/pmp-project-management-professional/pmp-value-based-delivery/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=pmp-study-guide)
  - Must know: Value is defined with the stakeholders who hold it, in measurable outcomes, before work is prioritized; reconcile competing definitions with them and the sponsor before escalating.
  - Must know: Intangible value such as trust or morale stays in the business case and is tracked through an agreed indirect measure with a baseline and target.
- [ ] **Plan and manage resources**
  <br>Enablers: Define and plan resources based on requirements; Manage and optimize resource needs and availability.
  - 📖 Lesson: [PMP Resource Management: Planning, Acquiring and Optimizing Project Resources](https://www.savemycert.com/revision/pmp-project-management-professional/pmp-plan-manage-project-resources/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=pmp-study-guide)
  - Must know: The resource management plan covers how team and physical resources are identified, acquired, developed, managed, recognized and released.
  - Must know: Resource needs are derived from requirements; when requirements change, update the resource plan and start acquisition ahead of the lead time.
- [ ] **Plan and manage procurement**
  <br>Enablers: Plan procurement; Execute a procurement management plan; Select preferred contract types; Evaluate vendor performance; Verify objectives of the procurement agreement are met; Participate in agreement negotiations; Determine a negotiation strategy; Manage suppliers and contracts; Plan and manage the procurement strategy; Develop a delivery solution.
  - 📖 Lesson: [PMP Procurement Management: Contract Types, Negotiation and Vendor Management](https://www.savemycert.com/revision/pmp-project-management-professional/pmp-procurement-management-contract-types/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=pmp-study-guide)
  - Must know: Make-or-buy comes before any bid document and counts opportunity cost and non-cost factors, not just the price.
  - Must know: RFQ for fully specified items compared on price; RFP when sellers must propose an approach; RFI to learn the market.
- [ ] **Plan and manage finance**
  <br>Enablers: Analyze project financial needs; Quantify risk and contingency financial allocations; Plan spend tracking throughout the project life cycle; Plan financial reporting; Anticipate future finance challenges; Monitor financial variations and work with the governance process; Manage financial reserves.
  - 📖 Lesson: [PMP Cost and Finance Management: Budget, Reserves and Earned Value](https://www.savemycert.com/revision/pmp-project-management-professional/pmp-project-cost-budget-financial-management/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=pmp-study-guide)
  - Must know: Analogous scales a past project's total, parametric multiplies a reliable unit rate by a quantity, and bottom-up rolls up work packages for the most accuracy.
  - Must know: Cost baseline = work package estimates + contingency reserve, and it equals BAC; project budget = cost baseline + management reserve.
- [ ] **Plan and optimize quality of products/deliverables**
  <br>Enablers: Gather quality requirements for project deliverables; Plan quality processes and tools; Execute a quality management plan; Help ensure regulatory compliance; Manage cost of quality (CoQ) and sustainability; Conduct ongoing quality reviews; Implement continuous improvement.
  - 📖 Lesson: [PMP Quality Management: Cost of Quality, Quality Tools and Continuous Improvement](https://www.savemycert.com/revision/pmp-project-management-professional/pmp-quality-management-cost-of-quality/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=pmp-study-guide)
  - Must know: Quality is meeting requirements; grade is the feature level. Low grade can be acceptable, low quality never is, and lowering grade is how to cut cost without cutting quality.
  - Must know: Gather quality requirements during planning, from every user group, regulations and standards, not only from functional acceptance criteria.
- [ ] **Plan and manage schedule**
  <br>Enablers: Prepare a schedule based on the selected development approach; Coordinate with other projects and operations; Estimate project tasks (milestones, dependencies, story points); Utilize benchmarks and historical data; Create a project schedule; Baseline a project schedule; Execute a schedule management plan; Analyze schedule variation.
  - 📖 Lesson: [PMP Schedule Management: Critical Path, Float, Baselines and Agile Forecasting](https://www.savemycert.com/revision/pmp-project-management-professional/pmp-schedule-management-critical-path/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=pmp-study-guide)
  - Must know: Detail follows certainty: network and critical path for stable, interdependent work; iterations or flow for changing work; milestones plus cadence for hybrid.
  - Must know: Iterations are fixed timeboxes: unfinished items go back to the product backlog rather than extending the iteration.
- [ ] **Evaluate project status**
  <br>Enablers: Develop project metrics, analysis, and reconciliation; Identify and tailor needed artifacts; Help ensure artifacts are created, reviewed, updated, and documented; Help ensure accessibility of artifacts; Assess current progress; Measure, analyze, and update project metrics; Communicate project status; Continually assess the effectiveness of artifact management.
  - 📖 Lesson: [PMP Project Status: Metrics, Earned Value, Burn Charts and Status Reporting](https://www.savemycert.com/revision/pmp-project-management-professional/pmp-evaluate-project-status-metrics-reporting/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=pmp-study-guide)
  - Must know: Outcome metrics show whether the project delivers value; output metrics only show activity. Add leading indicators when stakeholders need early warning.
  - Must know: Velocity is a single team's planning measure: count only accepted work, never turn it into a quota and never use it to rank teams.
- [ ] **Manage project closure**
  <br>Enablers: Obtain project stakeholder approval of project completion; Determine criteria to successfully close the project or phase; Validate readiness for transition (e.g., to operations team or next phase); Conclude activities to close the project or phase (e.g., final lessons learned, retrospectives, procurement, financials, resources).
  - 📖 Lesson: [PMP Project Closure: Completion Criteria, Transition, Lessons Learned and Closing Contracts](https://www.savemycert.com/revision/pmp-project-management-professional/pmp-project-closure-lessons-learned-transition/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=pmp-study-guide)
  - Must know: Closure criteria are measurable, agreed during planning by the sponsor, customer and receiving owner, and limited to conditions the project can meet before it ends; long-term benefits are tracked after closure.
  - Must know: When a stakeholder disputes completion, review the agreed criteria and the evidence with them; new expectations raised at closure are changes or follow-on work.

### Domain 3: Business Environment

**Weight: 26%.** Connecting the project to the organization: governance, compliance, change control, impediments and issues, risk, continuous improvement, organizational change and the external environment. Official weighting 26%.

📝 Revision notes: [Domain 3: Business Environment](notes/domain-3-business-environment.md)

- [ ] **Define and establish project governance**
  <br>Enablers: Describe and establish the structure, rules, procedures, reporting, ethics, and policies through the use of organizational process assets (OPAs); Define success metrics; Outline governance escalation paths and thresholds.
  - 📖 Lesson: [PMP Project Governance: Decision Rights, Escalation Thresholds and Success Metrics](https://www.savemycert.com/revision/pmp-project-management-professional/pmp-project-governance-escalation-success-metrics/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=pmp-study-guide)
  - Must know: Governance decides who decides, who oversees and what the project manager may decide alone; project management plans and delivers within it.
  - Must know: Start from the organization's process assets (policies, templates, procedures, lessons learned) and tailor them; culture and regulations are environmental factors, not OPAs.
- [ ] **Plan and manage project compliance**
  <br>Enablers: Confirm project compliance requirements (e.g., security, health and safety, sustainability, regulatory compliance); Classify compliance categories; Determine potential threats to compliance; Use methods to support compliance; Analyze the consequences of noncompliance; Determine the necessary approach and action(s) to address compliance needs; Measure the extent to which the project is in compliance.
  - 📖 Lesson: [PMP Project Compliance Management: Requirements, Threats, Evidence and Noncompliance](https://www.savemycert.com/revision/pmp-project-management-professional/pmp-project-compliance-management/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=pmp-study-guide)
  - Must know: Confirm compliance requirements early with qualified legal, compliance, safety or privacy experts; earlier lists, the sponsor's view or the team's own research are inputs, not confirmation.
  - Must know: Re-confirm obligations whenever scope adds a new jurisdiction, site or type of data, before the change is finalized.
- [ ] **Manage and control changes**
  <br>Enablers: Execute the change control process; Communicate the status of proposed changes; Implement approved changes to the project; Update project documentation to reflect changes.
  - 📖 Lesson: [PMP Integrated Change Control: Change Requests, CCB, Agile Change and Configuration Management](https://www.savemycert.com/revision/pmp-project-management-professional/pmp-integrated-change-control-process/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=pmp-study-guide)
  - Must know: Every proposed change to approved scope, schedule, cost or design becomes a documented change request in the change log, whoever asks and however.
  - Must know: Analyse first: no change reaches a decision-maker without an integrated impact analysis covering every affected stream and the risks of any recovery tactic.
- [ ] **Remove impediments and manage issues**
  <br>Enablers: Evaluate the impact of impediments; Prioritize and highlight impediments; Determine and apply an intervention strategy to remove/minimize impediments; Reassess continually to help ensure impediments, obstacles, and blockers for the team are being addressed; Recognize when a risk becomes an issue; Collaborate with relevant stakeholders on an approach to resolve the issues.
  - 📖 Lesson: [PMP Impediments and Issues: Remove Blockers, Escalate and Resolve](https://www.savemycert.com/revision/pmp-project-management-professional/pmp-remove-impediments-manage-issues/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=pmp-study-guide)
  - Must know: Evaluate first: find out what an impediment blocks, for how long and whether float absorbs it before choosing a response.
  - Must know: A delay beyond an activity's float can create a new critical path; parallel path delays do not add up, the milestone moves by the largest net delay.
- [ ] **Plan and manage risk**
  <br>Enablers: Identify risks; Analyze risks; Monitor and control risks; Develop a risk management plan; Maintain a risk register (e.g., poor IT security); Execute a risk management plan (e.g., risk response for security and managing sustainability risks); Communicate the status of a risk impact on the project.
  - 📖 Lesson: [PMP Risk Management: Plan, Register, Analysis and Responses](https://www.savemycert.com/revision/pmp-project-management-professional/pmp-risk-management-plan-register-responses/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=pmp-study-guide)
  - Must know: The risk management plan sets the approach (methodology, roles, funding, timing, categories, scales, thresholds, reporting); the register holds each risk with its owner, trigger, response and status.
  - Must know: Thresholds decide which risks need active responses: a zero-tolerance rule overrides a low matrix score, and a missed confidence target calls for responses to overall project risk.
- [ ] **Continuous improvement**
  <br>Enablers: Utilize lessons learned; Help ensure continuous improvement processes are updated; Update organizational process assets (OPAs).
  - 📖 Lesson: [PMP Continuous Improvement: Lessons Learned, Process Updates and Organizational Process Assets](https://www.savemycert.com/revision/pmp-project-management-professional/pmp-continuous-improvement-lessons-learned-opas/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=pmp-study-guide)
  - Must know: OPAs (templates, checklists, procedures, policies, lessons repositories, historical data) are the organization's own assets and can be updated by their owners; EEFs (culture, regulation, markets, infrastructure, purchased databases) are conditions the project responds to.
  - Must know: Review lessons and historical data from similar work before plans are baselined, and let evidence override an unsupported estimate.
- [ ] **Support organizational change**
  <br>Enablers: Assess organizational culture; Evaluate the impact of organizational change on the project and determine required actions.
  - 📖 Lesson: [PMP Organizational Change: Assessing Culture and Responding to Change](https://www.savemycert.com/revision/pmp-project-management-professional/pmp-organizational-change-and-culture/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=pmp-study-guide)
  - Must know: Assess culture by watching and asking how decisions really happen; when surveys and behavior disagree, believe the behavior.
  - Must know: A pattern repeated in past projects' lessons learned is culture to plan around, not bad luck.
- [ ] **Evaluate external business environment changes**
  <br>Enablers: Survey changes to the external business environment (e.g., regulations, technology, geopolitical, market); Assess and prioritize the impact on project scope/backlog based on changes in the external business environment; Continually review the external business environment for impacts on project scope/backlog.
  - 📖 Lesson: [PMP External Business Environment: Surveying and Assessing Outside Changes](https://www.savemycert.com/revision/pmp-project-management-professional/pmp-external-business-environment-changes/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=pmp-study-guide)
  - Must know: External changes come from outside the organization (laws, technology, geopolitics, markets); reorganizations, staffing and internal process changes are internal and handled by other tasks.
  - Must know: Survey outward with a structured scan such as PESTLE, fed by the experts who watch each area; classify by the kind of thing that changed (a change in preferences is Social whatever its subject).

## How to study for PMP

1. **Read the lesson for each topic** in the checklist above, starting with the heaviest domain. Every lesson is free on the [PMP revision notes](https://www.savemycert.com/revision/pmp-project-management-professional/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=pmp-study-guide).
2. **Practice straight after reading.** Answer [PMP practice questions](https://www.savemycert.com/practice/pmp-project-management-professional/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=pmp-study-guide) on the topic you just read. Each option comes with an explanation of why it is right or wrong.
3. **Review what you got wrong**, re-read that lesson section, and tick the topic off only when you get its questions right.
4. **Take a full-length [PMP mock exam](https://www.savemycert.com/mocks/pmp-project-management-professional/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=pmp-study-guide)** under the real time limit. Aim to pass mocks comfortably before you book.
5. **On the last day**, skim the [PMP cheat sheet](https://www.savemycert.com/cheat-sheet/pmp-project-management-professional/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=pmp-study-guide) instead of starting anything new.
