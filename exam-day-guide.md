# PMP Exam-Day Guide

This page covers the logistics of the Project Management Professional (PMP) exam: eligibility, booking, the online and test-center options, ID and check-in, the exam format, results, and what happens if you need another attempt. Every fact links to the official Project Management Institute (PMI) document it came from. PMI revises its policies, so confirm anything time-sensitive on the linked page before your appointment.

> Also see the [PMP practice questions](https://www.savemycert.com/practice/pmp-project-management-professional/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=pmp-study-guide) and [timed mock exams](https://www.savemycert.com/mocks/pmp-project-management-professional/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=pmp-study-guide).

## Eligibility before you can book

You apply to PMI before you can sit the exam. The application records two things:

- **Experience leading and directing projects** in the past 10 years, counted in non-overlapping months. The minimum depends on your education: 60 months with upper-secondary school, 48 months with an associate's-level or similar program, 36 months with a bachelor's degree or higher, and 24 months with a degree from a program accredited by PMI's Global Accreditation Center (GAC). The work does not have to be paid, but it must be in a professional setting. School projects, personal events and home improvement do not count.
- **At least 35 hours of project management training** aligned with the Examination Content Outline. Active CAPM holders have this requirement waived. PMI states that books and practice exams alone are not accepted as eligible training, so this study guide and SaveMyCert's practice material do not count toward the 35 hours.

Any application can be selected for audit. If yours is, PMI asks for copies of your degree, supervisor signatures for the experience you listed, and training certificates, and gives you 90 days to submit them.

Source: [PMP Examination Content Outline – July 2026](https://www.pmi.org/-/media/pmi/documents/public/pdf/certifications/new-pmp-examination-content-outline-2026.pdf), [PMI Certification Handbook](https://www.pmi.org/-/media/pmi/documents/public/pdf/certifications/generic-certification-handbook.pdf)

## Paying and booking the exam

PMI's handbook says certification fees vary by membership status and region, and member pricing applies only if you joined PMI before paying. Check the current price on pmi.org for your situation. This guide does not quote a figure because the amount could not be confirmed on an official page.

After you pay, PMI emails your **PMI Eligibility ID**, which you need to schedule. You then have a **1-year eligibility period** to take the exam. If your application was audited, that year starts when you are told the audit is complete.

Your eligibility letter shows your name as it will appear on exam day. If it needs to change, contact PMI as soon as possible; PMI cannot guarantee a name update within 5 business days of the exam.

Source: [PMI Certification Handbook](https://www.pmi.org/-/media/pmi/documents/public/pdf/certifications/generic-certification-handbook.pdf)

## Rescheduling, canceling and refunds

- You can reschedule or cancel up to **48 hours** before the appointment. Within 48 hours you cannot, and missing the exam forfeits the whole fee.
- Rescheduling or canceling **within 30 days** of the appointment costs **US$70 plus taxes**. The 30 days do not include exam day.
- A refund can be requested within 30 days of purchase if you have not scheduled and taken the exam in that time.
- Emergencies such as a medical emergency or a death in the family may qualify under PMI's extenuating circumstances policy. Work-related reasons do not.

To switch between online and test-center delivery, cancel the current appointment and choose the new type when you reschedule.

Source: [PMI Certification Handbook](https://www.pmi.org/-/media/pmi/documents/public/pdf/certifications/generic-certification-handbook.pdf)

## Test center or online

The PMP can be taken in person at a test center (computer-based, or paper-based where that applies) or online with a proctor through Pearson VUE OnVUE. In mainland China delivery is paper-based only, and the online option is not available to citizens of the People's Republic of China, Macau and Hong Kong.

- **Test center:** check in 30 minutes before your appointment. Late arrivals are not admitted. You sign in, show ID, give your PMI Eligibility ID, and have your photo and signature taken. You are wanded with a metal detector, and everything except your ID and locker key goes in a locker. You get scratch paper and pencils or marker boards. A calculator is built into the exam, and you can ask for a handheld one.
- **Online:** expect system tests and a longer check-in. To schedule online you must first pass a pre-scheduling identity verification; if you fail it, you are directed to book in person. You may be asked to show your ID again after a break. Run Pearson VUE's OnVUE system checks before exam day.

Source: [PMP Examination Content Outline – July 2026](https://www.pmi.org/-/media/pmi/documents/public/pdf/certifications/new-pmp-examination-content-outline-2026.pdf), [PMI Certification Handbook](https://www.pmi.org/-/media/pmi/documents/public/pdf/certifications/generic-certification-handbook.pdf)

## What to have ready

Bring a valid, unexpired, original **government-issued ID** that shows your photograph and your signature, with English (Roman) characters or an official English translation. If you test in the country that issued it, online or at a center, the ID may be in local characters; if you test outside that country, only Roman-character IDs are accepted. If your ID lacks a photo or signature, a current secondary ID that supplies the missing item and shows your name can be used. The name must match your scheduling notification exactly, and damaged IDs are refused. If you are turned away for ID, you must pay again to test later.

Source: [PMI Certification Handbook](https://www.pmi.org/-/media/pmi/documents/public/pdf/certifications/generic-certification-handbook.pdf)

## How the exam is structured

- **180 questions in 240 minutes.** 170 are scored and 10 are unscored pretest questions placed randomly, so treat every question as if it counts.
- **Two 10-minute breaks:** the first after the case-study section, the second about midway through the independent questions. Once you review your answers and start a break, you cannot go back to the previous section.
- An optional tutorial before the exam and a survey after it do not count against the 240 minutes. At a test center, the tutorial ends automatically after 15 minutes.
- **Question types:** multiple choice (single response), multiple response, case or scenario sets, graphic-based questions, enhanced matching, matching, point and click (hotspot), and pull-down lists. Some types (enhanced matching, matching, point and click, pull-down lists) appear only on computer-based tests at a center.
- **Approach mix:** about 40% of items are predictive and the rest are adaptive/agile or hybrid, spread across all three domains.
- **Domains:** People (33%), Process (41%), Business Environment (26%).
- The PMP is offered in English and several translated languages. A translated exam has an exhibit button that shows each question in English.

Source: [PMP Examination Content Outline – July 2026](https://www.pmi.org/-/media/pmi/documents/public/pdf/certifications/new-pmp-examination-content-outline-2026.pdf), [PMI Certification Handbook](https://www.pmi.org/-/media/pmi/documents/public/pdf/certifications/generic-certification-handbook.pdf)

## Scoring and results

PMI's handbook gives no numeric passing score. Its exams are criteria-based: the passing standard is set by psychometric analysis of what a qualified practitioner should be able to do, and only scored items count. After a computer-based exam you receive a score report with your pass or fail status and diagnostic information for each domain.

For the PMP, the result shown at the end of your session is **preliminary**. Your official exam report is available online no later than 10 business days after the exam, and PMI emails you when it is ready. Paper-based candidates receive their report within 6 to 8 weeks.

After passing, you can download your certificate from PMI's certification site, and Credly emails you a digital badge on PMI's behalf. Your name is listed in PMI's online Certification Registry unless you opt out.

Source: [PMI Certification Handbook](https://www.pmi.org/-/media/pmi/documents/public/pdf/certifications/generic-certification-handbook.pdf)

## If you don't pass

You can take the exam up to **three times within your 1-year eligibility period**, paying again for the second and third attempts. After three unsuccessful attempts you must wait 1 year from your last exam before reapplying for the PMP, though you can apply for other PMI certifications in that time. If your eligibility year runs out before you pass, you reapply, with no waiting period.

Source: [PMP Examination Content Outline – July 2026](https://www.pmi.org/-/media/pmi/documents/public/pdf/certifications/new-pmp-examination-content-outline-2026.pdf), [PMI Certification Handbook](https://www.pmi.org/-/media/pmi/documents/public/pdf/certifications/generic-certification-handbook.pdf)

## Keeping the certification

The PMP is maintained through PMI's Continuing Certification Requirements (CCR) program: earn and submit **60 professional development units (PDUs) every 3 years** and pay the renewal fee.

Source: [PMP Examination Content Outline – July 2026](https://www.pmi.org/-/media/pmi/documents/public/pdf/certifications/new-pmp-examination-content-outline-2026.pdf), [Maintain your certification](https://www.pmi.org/certifications/certification-resources/maintain)

## On exam day: practical tips

- **Pace for 180.** 240 minutes for 180 questions is about 80 seconds each. Check your pace at each break.
- **Finish each section before the break.** You cannot return to a section after starting its break, so review flagged questions first.
- **Read for the decisive detail.** Most questions offer several workable actions. Who holds the authority, whether a baseline is approved, and whether the work is predictive or agile usually decide the answer.
- **Understand before acting.** Options that analyze the situation, involve the people affected, and follow the agreed process usually beat options that escalate early, act alone, or bypass change control.
